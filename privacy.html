Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Drivo lets you customize your car's screen with widgets and a connection sound. It has no account, no ads and no tracking. Almost everything you create stays on your iPhone.

Data that stays on your device

Your photos, sounds, recordings, first name, car nickname, settings and onboarding answers are stored on your iPhone, in a space shared only between the Drivo app and its widgets. We don't receive them and we don't keep a copy. Deleting Drivo deletes them.

The camera, photo library and microphone are used only when you choose to take or pick a photo, or record a sound. The voice greeting uses your iPhone's built-in voices, on the device.

Weather and location

If you turn on the weather, Drivo asks for your location while you use the app. The position is rounded to about 1 km, then sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to get the current temperature. The last rounded position is kept on your iPhone so the weather can refresh in the background. Apple's handling of this request is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn location off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. To know whether your subscription is active, Drivo uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier, your purchase history for Drivo and basic technical data (device model, iOS version, app version, IP address). See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

The reminder before the end of your free trial is scheduled on your iPhone. No notification data is sent to us.

What we don't do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics tools.
  • We never sell or rent your data.

How long data is kept

Data on your iPhone is kept until you delete it or delete the app. RevenueCat keeps subscription records for as long as needed to provide your subscription and meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR in the European Union), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. Because Drivo has no account, the only data we can act on is the subscription record linked to your anonymous app ID. Write to us and we'll help. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Children

Drivo is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
